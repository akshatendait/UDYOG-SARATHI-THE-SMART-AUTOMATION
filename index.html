The Problem

Industrial approvals today are scattered across departments, portals and paperwork.

A single new unit can need consents from the pollution board, a factory licence, fire NOC, labour registration and an electricity connection — each with its own portal, timeline and document format. Udyog Sarathi brings them into one guided, trackable journey.

One reference ID, every stage

Every approval — from Consent to Establish through to the Factory Licence — is tracked under one applicant profile, so nothing gets lost between departments.

Smart document checklists

Select an approval type and instantly see exactly which documents are required — no guesswork, no rejected filings for missing paperwork.

Live status, real ETAs

Every application shows its current stage, an expected completion date, and officer remarks the moment they're recorded.

Matched government schemes

Based on your unit's profile, Udyog Sarathi surfaces subsidies, credit guarantees and skilling programmes you're eligible for.

Built for every applicant

High-contrast mode, adjustable text size, mouse-over assistance, and hover-to-read-aloud make the whole filing journey accessible for all.

Digital approval journey

Five connected stages, one workflow engine.

Hover or click a stage to see what happens at that step. Each stage is tracked, timestamped and audit-logged.

Live application tracking

Follow every stage in real time.

A live-feeling timeline of your application — with the current stage highlighted and completed stages confirmed.

Built around one reference ID

Every consent, licence and registration is attached to a single application reference. As a stage completes, your dashboard updates automatically — no chasing departments.
